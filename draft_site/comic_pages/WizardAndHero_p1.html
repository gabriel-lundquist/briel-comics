Text Description

Panel 1: Ayotli, a short young woman with a big round nose, peeks through a door frame. Ayotli: "Hello?" Panel 2: Various containers, devices, and tools are spread over the floor and several counters. The room is cramped for all the stuff in it. Ayotli: "Whoa." Panel 3: Zissel, a tall, thin woman with a long braid and a pointy nose, rigidly strides into view from around a corner. Zissel: "Hey! You're candidate H5, right?" Panel 4: Ayotli has short hair and wears a tank top, emphasizing her shoulders. Dismayed, she hunches and reaches out a hand to Zissel, looking up at her face. Zissel herself is leaning forward with her hands on her hips, expectant. Ayotli: "Um. I'm Ayotli." Panel 5: Zissel reciprocates the dap. Both she and Ayotli stand straighter and relax their faces. Zissel: "Okay. Ayotli. I'm Wizard Zissel."
