Text description of Drink page 2

Page 2 of "Drink". The pilgrim says, "I want to value myself without having to feel special." The squid says, "Ah. Well, sadsack ape or not I got spirits for you." They reaches for a bottle, which appears prideful. "Here's, uh, platonic lotus-wine. You may detect notes of an ontological understanding of yourself as an endless four-dimensional pattern in spacetime, supremely and cosmically unique. In the back of your throat that sublime uniqueness becomes a pit of alienation from which no feeling or truth can escape." Next they pulls out a blocky bottle, surrounded by a regular pattern of other vessels. "Oh and this is malt of primordium. About two fingers, depending on the brand, has you feeling your intrinsic oneness with every object in reality as well as the insignificant gasping death of your own ego. Don't drink it if you're not prepared to lose track of which one of us is you. High-shelf labels pay a chorus to sing hymns of ancient gods into the still. The words condense into small black cubes that sit at the bottom of your glass like boba." A rocks glass shows these cubes, one of which says "Wiraqutra", the other of which says "Ninkasi".
