Search options

  • Enter -something to exclude any pages that contain "something"
  • Enter title:tuesday to get pages with "tuesday" in their titles, as opposed to just searching tuesday which gets you any page posted on a tuesday. Valid prefixes are:
    • tag
    • cw (as in, Content Warning)
    • title
    • day (searches both day of the month and day of the week)
    • month
    • year
    • description
  • You can use both at once, like -cw:gore if you're okay with the word gore but you don't want to actually see it.
  • Check "Search image descriptions too" and uncheck "Match terms exactly" if you're looking for a specific comic but you can't remember exact dialogue. The machine will attempt to find image descriptions that have similar words (no AI, just MySQL's fulltext search algorithm).
